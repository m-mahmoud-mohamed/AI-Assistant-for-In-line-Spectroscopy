A multispectral fiber probe, e.g. one combining MIR-ATR and fluorescence techniques into a single 
fiber optic probe shaft allows for the simultaneous acquisition of complementary chemical information 
from the exact same measurement point, a capability critical for analyzing heterogeneous solid samples 
like biological tissues or rapidly changing liquid environments.
