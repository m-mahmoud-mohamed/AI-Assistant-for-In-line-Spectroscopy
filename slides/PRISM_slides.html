Approach

A sales engineer's first assessment: grounded and honest

The task

  • Read a free-text enquiry: process, sample, targets, constraints.
  • Ask what is missing; recommend method, spectral range and probe concept.
  • Justify with the AFS materials; risks; first feasibility study.

Why not a plain chatbot

  • Invents specifications and performance figures.
  • Fills in missing client facts; quotes lab studies as proof.
  • Sounds confident where "not enough information" is right.

PRISM's principles

  • Rules decide, the LLM writes. Deterministic engine owns status, candidates, blocked methods.
  • AFS materials first. AFS claims cite a card whose quote is verbatim on the page.
  • Typed statements: client fact · AFS · external · assumption · proposal · unknown.
  • Ask before guessing: questions ranked by how much the answer changes the recommendation.
  • Verify every sentence before the scientist sees it.

Example recommendation · H04

"Can the assistant approve this concept?" Not yet.

Enquiry: water content in a petroleum line using MIR ATR; analyzer in another building; reuse a silica fiber from a VIS/NIR setup; link length and conditions not supplied.

INSUFFICIENT INFORMATION NOT YET

There is not enough information to approve the MIR ATR concept as proposed.

CONTRADICTED reuse of the silica fiber:

VIS–NIR silica transmits about 350–2400 nm, which does not overlap the MIR range of 4000–400 cm⁻¹ (2.5–25 µm).

Concept

  • MIR ATR probe (conditional) and screening of candidate methods on retained samples.
  • Fiber: polycrystalline (AgHal) IR fiber or hollow glass waveguide; long link to be confirmed with AFS.

First questions to the client

  • What does the liquid look like at the measurement point?
  • Cable route and length to the other building?
  • Research, pilot or production use?

Example feasibility plan · H02

Two dairy streams, two probe geometries, one comparison study

Recommendation

  • Clear whey: NIR transmission / transflection probe (primary).
  • Opaque concentrate: NIR diffuse reflection probe (primary).
  • NIR 780–2500 nm; VIS-NIR silica fiber; link length to be confirmed.
Compare the specified NIR probe geometries on the same representative samples from each stream before selecting the installation.

First feasibility study (≤ 6 steps)

  1. Clarify ranges, acceptance criteria and installation constraints.
  2. Collect representative samples across agreed compositions and physical states.
  3. Obtain time-aligned laboratory references.
  4. Set up interfaces; compare spectra and models per stream.
  5. Validate on independent production material.
  6. Go / no-go against pre-agreed criteria.

Limitations and next steps

What PRISM does not do yet

Limitations

  • Coverage: 71 curated cards; 129 drafted cards await expert review; S05/S06 are business context only.
  • Support strength: under half of judged claims are fully supported by their quote; the rest are hedged or marked.
  • Premise verdicts can still differ from an expert's (1 of 7 on the development cases); the evaluation set is small (8 gold cases).
  • No measured spectra, no OCR; prototype without user accounts; enquiry text goes to the chosen LLM provider.

Next steps

  • Expert review of drafted cards; page-level AFS retrieval.
  • Calibrate the judge on expert labels (tooling in place).
  • Attach measured spectra from the AFS lab to the plan.
  • Accounts, roles and an on-premise model for NDA enquiries.
