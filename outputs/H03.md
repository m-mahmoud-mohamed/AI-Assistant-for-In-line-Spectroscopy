## Recommendation
- **NIR · transmission or transflection probe** — PRIMARY, evidence Moderate. NIR has documented application to tracking major components such as glucose.[^S01-p16-nir-major-components] Transmission or transflection probes are listed for liquid and colloidal samples.[^TABLE-nir_transmission_or_transflection_probe]
  - Conditions: Quantitative NIR analysis requires chemometric calibration because its broad absorption bands overlap. _(assumption)_
  - Limitations: NIR may lack the sensitivity needed for trace components.[^S01-p16-nir-major-components] Whether NIR can report viable biomass in this fermentation remains unknown. _(unknown)_
- **Raman · probe** — COMPARISON, evidence Moderate. Raman is suited to aqueous media because water interferes less than in infrared methods.[^S01-p16-raman-aqueous] The weak Raman effect requires a specialised probe.[^S01-p05-raman-weak]
  - Conditions: The aqueous-medium rationale assumes that the fermentation liquid is water-based, which must be confirmed. _(assumption)_
  - Limitations: Fluorescence background can overwhelm Raman signals from many organic compounds.[^S01-p11-raman-fluorescence]
- **UV-Vis · ATR probe** — COMPARISON, evidence Moderate. UV-Vis provides a basis for comparison, as suspension turbidity correlates with cell density.[^S01-p16-uvvis-biomass]
  - Limitations: Turbidity correlates with cell density.[^S01-p16-uvvis-biomass] Whether the specified ATR geometry can provide a useful signal for the requested targets remains unknown. _(unknown)_

## Spectral range
- NIR: 780–2500 nm; fiber: VIS-NIR silica fiber. The nominal NIR range is 780–2500 nm.[^S01-p10-nir-range] The usable range depends on the instrument, fiber and probe and is specified for the selected system. Quantitative NIR analysis needs chemometric calibration to address overlapping bands. _(assumption)_
- Raman: Raman shift in cm⁻¹; detection region 400–1100 nm; fiber: UV-VIS silica fiber, VIS-NIR silica fiber. Raman detection happens in the 400–1100 nm visible/short-wave NIR region.[^S01-p11-raman-region] Raman spectra are reported as Raman shift in cm⁻¹, separate from the excitation wavelength.[^S01-p11-raman-shift] The usable range depends on the instrument, fiber and probe and is specified for the selected system. State the excitation wavelength separately from the Raman shift and choose it with the sample's fluorescence and the instrument in mind. Fluorescence background can overwhelm Raman signals from many organic compounds.[^S01-p11-raman-fluorescence]
- UV-Vis: 190–780 nm; fiber: UV-VIS silica fiber. The UV region used by UV-Vis spectroscopy is 190–360 nm.[^S01-p05-uv-range] The nominal NIR range is 780–2500 nm.[^S01-p10-nir-range] The usable range depends on the instrument, fiber and probe and is specified for the selected system. Strong overlap of UV signals complicates the analysis of real samples.[^S01-p05-uv-overlap]
