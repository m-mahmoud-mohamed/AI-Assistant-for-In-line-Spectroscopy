## Recommendation
- **NIR · transmission or transflection probe** — PRIMARY, evidence Moderate. NIR has application support for tracking major components such as glucose.[^S01-p16-nir-major-components]
  - Conditions: Because broad absorption bands overlap, quantitative NIR analysis suggests the use of chemometrics for quantitative and qualitative analysis of spectral data.[^S01-p10-nir-chemometrics]
  - Limitations: NIR may lack sensitivity for trace components.[^S01-p16-nir-major-components] Whether NIR can report viable biomass in this fermentation is unknown. _(unknown)_
- **Raman · probe** — COMPARISON, evidence Moderate. Raman is attractive for aqueous media because water interferes less than in infrared methods.[^S01-p16-raman-aqueous]
  - Limitations: The weak Raman signal requires special probes.[^S01-p05-raman-weak] Fluorescence background can overwhelm the Raman signal of many organic compounds.[^S01-p11-raman-fluorescence]
- **UV-Vis · ATR probe** — COMPARISON, evidence Moderate. Compare the UV-Vis ATR response with the requested variables rather than assume a direct assay. Strong overlap of UV spectra limits selectivity in real mixtures.[^S01-p05-uv-overlap]
  - Limitations: Do not equate optical density or turbidity with viable biomass.
- **Fluorescence · fluorimetric probe** — NOT_RECOMMENDED, evidence Blocked. Fluorimetry may require a defined fluorescent species or a justified fluorescent indicator.[^S01-p05-fluorescence-not-general][^S01-p16-fluorescence-targets]

## Spectral range
- NIR: 780–2500 nm; fiber: VIS-NIR silica fiber. The nominal NIR range is 780–2500 nm.[^S01-p10-nir-range] The usable range depends on the instrument, fiber and probe and is specified for the selected system. Overlapping NIR absorption bands suggest the use of chemometrics for quantitative and qualitative analysis of spectral data.[^S01-p10-nir-chemometrics]
- Raman: Raman shift in cm⁻¹; detection region 400–1100 nm; fiber: UV-VIS silica fiber, VIS-NIR silica fiber. Raman detection happens in the 400–1100 nm visible/short-wave NIR region.[^S01-p11-raman-region] Raman spectra are reported as Raman shift in cm⁻¹, separate from the excitation wavelength.[^S01-p11-raman-shift] The usable range depends on the instrument, fiber and probe and is specified for the selected system. State the excitation wavelength separately from the Raman shift and choose it with the sample's fluorescence and the instrument in mind. Fluorescence background can overwhelm Raman signals from many organic compounds.[^S01-p11-raman-fluorescence]
- UV-Vis: 190–780 nm; fiber: UV-VIS silica fiber. The UV region used by UV-Vis spectroscopy is 190–360 nm.[^S01-p05-uv-range] The nominal NIR range is 780–2500 nm.[^S01-p10-nir-range] The usable range depends on the instrument, fiber and probe and is specified for the selected system. Strong overlap of UV signals limits selectivity in real mixtures.[^S01-p05-uv-overlap]
