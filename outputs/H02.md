## Recommendation
- **NIR · transmission or transflection probe** — PRIMARY, evidence Strong. NIR transmission is preferred for transparent or semi-transparent liquids such as whey.[^S01-p18-nir-transmission-clear]
  - Conditions: Quantitative NIR analysis may need chemometric calibration because its absorption bands overlap.[^S01-p10-nir-chemometrics]
- **NIR · diffuse reflection probe** — PRIMARY, evidence Strong. In-line NIR reflection is used for opaque or highly concentrated dairy products, including milk concentrates.[^S01-p17-nir-reflection-opaque]
  - Limitations: NIR reflection is sensitive to surface characteristics and fat globule size.[^S01-p17-reflection-surface]
- **NIR · diffuse reflection probe** — CONDITIONAL, evidence Strong. NIR diffuse-reflection probes are suited to scattering media.[^TABLE-nir_diffuse_reflection_probe]
  - Conditions: Retain this option for whey only if the sample comparison demonstrates a usable diffuse-reflection signal.
- **NIR · transmission or transflection probe** — CONDITIONAL, evidence Weak. A transflection probe can sometimes serve both low-fat and high-fat milk products.[^S01-p18-transflection-combined]
  - Conditions: Retain this option for the concentrate only if sample tests demonstrate usable transmission or transflection spectra.
  - Limitations: Transmission fails when milk becomes too thick or fat-rich for light to penetrate.[^S01-p18-transmission-fails-thick]

## Risks and uncertainty
- It fails when the milk becomes too "thick" or fat-rich, as the light cannot penetrate the sample.[^S01-p18-transmission-fails-thick] Effect: Conditional concentrate interface may be unusable. Mitigation: Check penetration before calibration.
- NIR reflection is sensitive to surface characteristics and fat globule size.[^S01-p17-reflection-surface] Effect: Physical variation may confound composition estimates. Mitigation: Include physical variation in the comparison.
- The extent of build-up at either measurement interface is unknown. _(unknown)_ Effect: Optical contact may change during operation. Mitigation: Confirm cleaning provisions with AFS.

- **References:** Pair spectra with time-aligned laboratory protein and fat results wherever those variables are relevant.

- **Validation:** Validate each model against laboratory results on independent production material excluded from model fitting and selection.
