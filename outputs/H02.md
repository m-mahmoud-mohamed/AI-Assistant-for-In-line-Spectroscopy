## Recommendation
- **NIR · transmission or transflection probe** — PRIMARY, evidence Strong. NIR transmission is preferred for transparent or semi-transparent liquids such as whey.[^S01-p18-nir-transmission-clear] NIR transmission or transflection probes are listed for liquid and colloidal samples.[^TABLE-nir_transmission_or_transflection_probe]
  - Conditions: Confirm adequate light transmission through representative whey samples at the proposed optical path.
  - Limitations: Overlapping NIR bands suggest the use of chemometrics for quantitative analysis.[^S01-p10-nir-chemometrics]
- **NIR · diffuse reflection probe** — PRIMARY, evidence Strong. In-line NIR reflection is used for opaque or highly concentrated dairy products, including whole milk concentrates.[^S01-p17-nir-reflection-opaque]
  - Limitations: NIR reflection is sensitive to surface characteristics and fat globule size.[^S01-p17-reflection-surface]
- **NIR · diffuse reflection probe** — CONDITIONAL, evidence Strong. NIR diffuse reflection probes are listed for scattering media.[^TABLE-nir_diffuse_reflection_probe]
  - Conditions: Retain diffuse reflection as an option for the whey only if sample tests demonstrate a usable diffuse-reflection signal.
- **NIR · transmission or transflection probe** — CONDITIONAL, evidence Weak. A transflection probe can sometimes provide a combined solution for low-fat and high-fat milk products.[^S01-p18-transflection-combined]
  - Conditions: Retain transmission or transflection for the concentrate only if sample tests demonstrate adequate light penetration.
  - Limitations: Transmission fails when milk becomes too thick or fat-rich for light to penetrate.[^S01-p18-transmission-fails-thick]

## Spectral range

The nominal NIR range is 780–2500 nm.[^S01-p10-nir-range] The usable range depends on the instrument, fiber and probe and is specified for the selected system. ## Risks and uncertainty
- Transmission fails when milk becomes too thick or fat-rich for light to penetrate.[^S01-p18-transmission-fails-thick] Effect: Potentially unusable transmission signal in concentrate. Mitigation: Check penetration before attempting calibration.
- NIR reflection is sensitive to surface characteristics and fat globule size.[^S01-p17-reflection-surface] Effect: Physical variation can confound composition estimates. Mitigation: Include physical variation in the interface comparison.

- **References:** Pair spectra with laboratory protein and fat results wherever each variable is relevant, matching sampling times.

- **Validation:** Validate each stream’s models on independent material excluded from calibration and model selection.
- **Go / no-go:** Make a separate go/no-go decision for each stream and relevant target using acceptance criteria agreed with you before testing.
