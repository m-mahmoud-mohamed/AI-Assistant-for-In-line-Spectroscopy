## Recommendation
- **NIR + Raman + UV-Vis · multi-method screening on retained samples** — SCREENING, evidence Weak. The LED NIR kidney study provides a proof-of-concept basis for investigating tumor discrimination, not clinical validation.[^S03-p14-proof-of-concept] Compare NIR, Raman and UV-Vis on retained samples before choosing a method for further research.
  - Limitations: Spectra from frozen/thawed biopsies can differ from measurements made in vivo.[^S03-p03-ex-vivo-caveat] Fluorescence background can overwhelm Raman signals from many organic compounds.[^S01-p11-raman-fluorescence]

## Measurement concept
- s1: NIR + Raman + UV-Vis multi-method screening on retained samples; measurement point to be agreed with the client; fiber link: VIS-NIR silica fiber, UV-VIS silica fiber; link length to be confirmed. First test whether the interface samples a representative volume across retained boundary regions, rather than relying on an isolated measurement. Confirm optical access, fouling control and cleaning compatibility with AFS for the research setup.

## Spectral range
- NIR: 780–2500 nm; fiber: VIS-NIR silica fiber. The nominal NIR range is 780–2500 nm.[^S01-p10-nir-range] The usable range depends on the instrument, fiber and probe and is specified for the selected system. The broad absorption bands observed in this region overlap, suggesting the use of chemometrics for quantitative and qualitative analysis of spectral data.[^S01-p10-nir-chemometrics]
- Raman: Raman shift in cm⁻¹; detection region 400–1100 nm; fiber: UV-VIS silica fiber, VIS-NIR silica fiber. Raman detection happens in the 400–1100 nm visible/short-wave NIR region.[^S01-p11-raman-region] Raman spectra are reported as Raman shift in cm⁻¹, separate from the excitation wavelength.[^S01-p11-raman-shift] The usable range depends on the instrument, fiber and probe and is specified for the selected system. State the excitation wavelength separately from the Raman shift and choose it with the sample's fluorescence and the instrument in mind. Fluorescence background can overwhelm the Raman signal of many organic compounds.[^S01-p11-raman-fluorescence]
- UV-Vis: 190–780 nm; fiber: UV-VIS silica fiber. The nominal NIR range is 780–2500 nm.[^S01-p10-nir-range] The usable range depends on the instrument, fiber and probe and is specified for the selected system.

## Risks and uncertainty
- The spatial correspondence between the sampled optical volume and the tumor boundary is unknown. _(unknown)_ Effect: Boundary location may be misinterpreted. Mitigation: Compare sampled volumes with reference findings.
- NIR reflection is sensitive to surface characteristics.[^S01-p17-reflection-surface] Effect: Surface variation may mimic boundary contrast. Mitigation: Assess surface variation during retained-sample screening.
- Assumption: Representative retained material spanning the intended boundary variation will be available. (confirm with Client)

## First feasibility experiment
**Objective:** Assess whether spectroscopy on retained samples warrants further research for tumor boundary assessment.

1. Clarify sample states, target range, references and acceptance criteria.
2. Collect representative retained samples.
3. Obtain matched reference measurements.
4. Set up and compare candidate methods at matched locations.
5. Validate on independent retained material.
6. Decide go or no-go for further research only.

- **Samples:** Use representative retained samples spanning the client-agreed target range and relevant sample states.
- **References:** Obtain matched comparison values for the sampled locations using the reference method agreed with the client.
- **Set-up:** Record NIR, Raman and UV-Vis spectra at matched sample locations under documented research conditions.
- **Comparison:** Compare each candidate with the agreed reference against criteria fixed with the client before testing.
- **Validation:** Validate the candidate approaches on retained material from patients excluded from model development.
- **Go / no-go:** Make meeting the pre-agreed criteria, including false-negative outcomes, a condition for proceeding to further research.
- **Not demonstrated:** Readiness for live surgical tumor boundary decisions would remain unestablished after this retained-sample feasibility study. _(unknown)_
