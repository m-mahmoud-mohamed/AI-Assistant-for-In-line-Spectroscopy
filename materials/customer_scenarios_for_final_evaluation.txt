AFS Hackathon 2026 - Customer scenarios for final evaluation (H01-H06)

The task and the evaluation criteria are defined in the challenge brief.
Develop your assistant on D01-D08 (AFS_Examples_and_Reference.docx), then
test it on the six enquiries below. The final evaluation uses exactly this
text.

- The enquiries are constructed from the supplied application material. They
  are not real AFS client records.
- Each enquiry contains everything the client has said. Anything not stated is
  unknown: ask about it or state it as an assumption, do not treat it as fact.

==============================================================================
H01 | A new formulation after an existing NIR pilot
==============================================================================

Our existing NIR pilot follows API concentration in one powder formulation at
the tablet press feed frame. We are introducing a different API and excipient
blend and would like to reuse the calibration. A colleague suggests adding
Raman so that the old calibration will remain valid. What should our first
technical assessment and experiment be?

==============================================================================
H02 | Composition monitoring across two dairy streams
==============================================================================

Our dairy process handles both a relatively clear whey stream and an opaque
milk concentrate. We want a pilot for composition monitoring, using laboratory
protein and fat results where those variables are relevant. We can compare
different measurement interfaces before choosing the installation. What first
measurement concepts and comparison experiment would make sense for these two
streams?

==============================================================================
H03 | A fluorescence instrument requested for glucose and viable biomass
==============================================================================

We already have a fluorescence spectrometer and would like to use it to report
glucose concentration and viable biomass directly during fermentation. Our
only justification is that fluorescence can be very sensitive. We have not
identified a fluorescent target, marker or reference relationship. What should
the assistant tell us?

==============================================================================
H04 | Reuse of a silica link for petroleum MIR ATR
==============================================================================

We want to investigate water content in a petroleum line using MIR ATR. The
analyzer would be in another building. We already have a silica fiber cable
from a visible or NIR measurement setup and would like to reuse it. The link
length, routing and operating conditions have not yet been supplied. Can the
assistant approve this concept?

==============================================================================
H05 | An undefined off specification liquid
==============================================================================

We have a liquid process that occasionally goes off specification. We suspect
contamination, but we cannot yet name the contaminant or the affected quality
variable. We would like you to recommend the best spectroscopy method and
exact sensor configuration immediately. What information and first experiment
should come before that decision?

==============================================================================
H06 | A research sensor requested for immediate surgical use
==============================================================================

We read the supplied LED NIR kidney study and want to use the same concept to
guide tumor boundary decisions during live surgery. Can we quote its findings
as proof that the proposed sensor is ready for that use? We have no new in
vivo data and no validation on our own setup. What assessment and next
feasibility steps are appropriate?
