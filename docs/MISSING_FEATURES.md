**Checked against:**
- the challenge brief, the examples document and the H-scenario file (traced in `docs/SOLUTION_PLAN.md` §8);
- the plan's gap list (§13) and MoSCoW scope (§9.3);
- the implementation plan (Task 32);

**Priorities:**

**Effort:** S is under 2 h, M is half a day, L is a day or more.
